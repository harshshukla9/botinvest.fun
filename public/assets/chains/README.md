# Chain marks

- `robinhood.svg` is the official feather mark served by the
  [Robinhood Chain documentation](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/).
- `solana.svg` is the official gradient mark from
  [Solana brand assets](https://solana.com/branding).

Robinhood and Solana names and marks remain the property of their respective
owners. They are used here only to identify the chain selected by the user.
